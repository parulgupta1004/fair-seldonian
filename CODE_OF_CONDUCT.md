# Code of Conduct

Fair-Seldonian is dedicated to creating a welcoming, supportive, and diverse
community around the project. We want every member of our community to thrive,
with a particular focus on supporting those from traditionally marginalized or
underrepresented groups.

## What this code of conduct defines

This Code of Conduct defines expected behavior and guidelines that help create
such a community. Anyone who participates in the Fair-Seldonian project, online
or in person, is expected to show respect and courtesy to others in all
interactions. These interactions may occur in a variety of locations, including:

- in the project's GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, and code review),
- in any other official project communication channel,
- when representing the project in public,
- or in any event, online or in person, where the project is represented.

To make sure that everyone has a common understanding of "show respect and
courtesy to each other," we have adopted the following code of conduct. It is
enforced by the project maintainers.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following types of behavior are unacceptable in Fair-Seldonian spaces, both
online and in person, and constitute code of conduct violations.

### Abusive behavior

- **Harassment:** including offensive verbal comments related to gender, sexual
  orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, or religion, as
  well as sexual images in public spaces, deliberate intimidation, stalking,
  following, harassing photography or recording, inappropriate physical contact,
  and unwelcome sexual or romantic attention.
- **Threats:** threatening someone physically or verbally. For example,
  threatening to publicize sensitive information about someone's personal life.

### Unwelcoming behavior

**Blatant acts and micro-aggressions.** Blatant micro-aggressions involve making
statements that assume negative characteristics in a blanket fashion because of
someone's identification with a particular group. We take micro-aggressions
seriously, especially when they target traditionally marginalized groups (e.g.,
explicitly racist, sexist, homophobic, or transphobic statements). For example,
arguing that some people are less intelligent because of their gender, race, or
religion.

**Repeated, subtle, and unintentional micro-aggressions.** Subtle acts of
aggression refer to small, unintentionally harmful mistakes made in
conversation. These acts are not necessarily a code of conduct violation when
made by mistake. However, repeating something after it has been pointed out to
you that you broke a social rule, or antagonizing or arguing with someone who has
pointed out your micro-aggression, is considered unwelcoming behavior and is not
allowed in the community.

**Maliciousness towards other community members.** Deliberately attempting to
make others feel bad, name-calling, or singling out others for derision or
exclusion. For example, telling someone they're not a real programmer or that
they don't belong. If somebody makes such a statement without malice, they may
still be in violation of the Code of Conduct if their actions are deemed
especially or repeatedly unpleasant. Being especially or repeatedly unpleasant —
for example, agitating, rude, or especially distracting behavior over an extended
period of time — is also a violation.

## Scope

Community members are held to the standards outlined in this code of conduct when
interacting in the project's GitHub repository, when interacting in person at
events where they could represent the project, and in any other official project
space.

In addition, the community and experience often extend outside those spaces.
Abusive or unwelcoming behavior between community members still has a profound
impact on individuals and on the community when it happens beyond our walls. The
maintainers will use their discretion when deciding how to respond to reports of
such behavior and how to proceed with enforcement.

> **Note:** When in doubt, please report unacceptable behavior to us. If
> someone's behavior makes you feel unsafe within the community, we want to
> support and help you.

## Enforcement

### Enforcement related to abusive behavior

We do not tolerate abusive behavior. If we witness or receive a report of abusive
behavior, we will contact the person to have a conversation with them and verify
what has transpired. If we verify abusive behavior:

- Permissions will be removed from any project-related repositories and accounts, and
- The person will not be welcome in any digital or physical spaces covered by
  this Code of Conduct.

### Enforcement related to unwelcoming behavior

If we verify unwelcoming but non-abusive behavior, we will have a conversation
with the person about their behavior. The goal of the conversation is to ensure
that they understand the expectation that they not repeat the behavior, or other
behaviors that would violate the Code of Conduct.

### Response protocol steps

The maintainers will use the steps below to respond to reports of code of conduct
violations:

1. The report will be read carefully.
2. We will follow up with the person who reported the violation.
3. We will follow up with the community member whose behavior was reported.
4. Based on the above steps, we will determine whether there has been a code of
   conduct violation and what response is appropriate.

## Reporting code of conduct violations

If you see a violation of this code of conduct, please report it to the project
maintainer, [@parulgupta1004](https://github.com/parulgupta1004), via GitHub.
All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainers are obligated to respect
the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

### When to seek help immediately

Instead of filing a code of conduct violation report, please contact law
enforcement directly to report criminal activity (e.g., physical assault, sexual
assault, theft), or to report a dangerous physical situation (e.g., fire, serious
injury, fear that someone will hurt themselves or someone else).
