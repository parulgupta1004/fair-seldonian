Added `examples/demographic_parity_guarantee.ipynb`, which walks the whole workflow in order: define demographic parity with the builder, choose `epsilon`, choose `delta`, train, and read the verdict. It opens by framing the scope honestly — `QSA` fits its own logistic regression rather than wrapping an arbitrary scikit-learn estimator, so the realistic comparison is an unconstrained `LogisticRegression` against a certified-fair model on the same arrays: 0.848 accuracy at a 0.315 parity gap, against 0.738 at 0.040. `epsilon` and `delta` are then varied one at a time, since they fail for different reasons even when they surface the same `failure_mode`. by @parulgupta1004
