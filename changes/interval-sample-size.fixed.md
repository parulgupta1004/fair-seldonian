**Confidence intervals used the wrong sample size.** The width was computed from `#{Y == 1}` across *all* groups while the estimate divided by one group's size. On a 500-member group inside 20,000 samples the interval came out about 4.5x too narrow and empirical coverage fell to roughly 0.5 against a nominal 0.95 — invisible on a balanced benchmark, where the two counts coincide. Estimate, sample size and variance now all derive from one contribution vector, so they cannot disagree. by @parulgupta1004
