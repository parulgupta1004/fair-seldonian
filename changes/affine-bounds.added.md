Added `constraints.affine`, selectable as `seldonian_type="affine"`, which compiles a constraint to a maximum of affine forms and bounds each with a *single* interval — exploiting the independence of disjoint groups — instead of wrapping an interval around every tree node. It spends `K` slices of `delta`, one per form, rather than one per leaf occurrence. On the paper constraint this roughly halves the slack; the tests pin at least a 25% reduction. Constraints outside the fragment raise `NotAffine` rather than falling back silently. by @parulgupta1004
