**Breaking:** `fair_seldonian.experiments` is rebuilt around a `StudySpec` dataclass and `run_study`, replacing `run_experiments` and its Ray driver with `ProcessPoolExecutor`. Three measurement fixes come with it: each trial now draws a fresh dataset, where one dataset was previously subsampled (measuring subsampling rather than sampling noise); violations are counted against the plug-in `true_g` rather than the `eval_ghat` upper bound, which understated them and read zero for every roughly-correct variant; and refusals are recorded as `NaN` rather than `0`, so they cannot be averaged into a curve. `summarise` reports Clopper–Pearson exact intervals and carries `n_solutions` alongside every conditional mean. by @parulgupta1004
