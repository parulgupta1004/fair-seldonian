**The candidate/safety split could disagree with itself by a row.** `X` and `Y` were split with `train_test_split(test_size=1-r)`, whose test size rounds up, while `T` was split with `np.split` at `int(r*n)`, which rounds down. The two disagree by one row at some ratios — 0.7 and 0.44 among them, though not the 0.4 default — leaving `T` a different length from the predictions and raising `IndexError` from the group mask, so those ratios could not be used at all. The boundary is now computed once, in `split_candidate_safety`. by @parulgupta1004
