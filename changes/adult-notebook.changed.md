`real_world_adult.ipynb` now uses the `PR` encoding via `demographic_parity`, reports `diagnostics.failure_mode` on refusal, and gains a section showing what tolerance *would* certify and what it costs. Its markdown previously explained demographic parity as the four-leaf `TP(1) FP(1) + ...` form, which is no longer the idiom. The new section is the point of it: certification arrives at `epsilon = 0.15`, but that model scores 0.479 against a predict-nobody baseline of 0.765 — certified and useless — and only by `epsilon = 0.30` does accuracy recover to roughly the constant-model level, by which point the tolerated gap is twice the disparity we started with.
